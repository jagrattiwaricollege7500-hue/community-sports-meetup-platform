About us and contact

PlayLocal is a community project that helps neighbours find sports they enjoy, meet new people and stay active. Anyone can join: kids, adults and seniors, beginners and regular players.

  • Free and open to everyone in the community
  • Volunteer organizers run every event
  • Safe, friendly sessions at local grounds and halls

Email: hello@playlocal.example
Open: 9 AM to 6 PM, Monday to Saturday
